🚀 Browse the Internet Faster

  • Close unnecessary browser tabs.
  • Clear unnecessary browser cache.
  • Keep your browser updated.
  • Remove unwanted browser extensions.
  • Use a reliable internet connection.

🔐 Internet Safety Tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Avoid downloading files from unknown websites.
  • Do not share passwords with anyone.
  • Use two-factor authentication when available.

🛠️ Common Internet Problems

Internet is slow

Restart your router or check whether other devices are using a large amount of internet data.

Website is not opening

Check your internet connection and try refreshing the page.

Browser is not responding

Close unnecessary tabs and restart the browser.

💡 Useful Browser Shortcuts

  • Ctrl + T — Open a new tab
  • Ctrl + W — Close current tab
  • Ctrl + L — Select address bar
  • Ctrl + R — Refresh page
  • Ctrl + Shift + T — Reopen closed tab
